# Safety

Iniva serves adolescent girls and young women, some of them minors. Safety rules:

1. **Crisis first.** Each message is checked for signs of sexual violence, violence, suicidal thoughts or a medical emergency before any answer. A support message and helpline contacts are shown immediately (data/safety.json).
2. **Age-appropriate access.** Users choose an age range; under-15s are directed to trusted adults and support contacts.
3. **No diagnosis, no doses.** Answers give general, validated information and refer to health workers.
4. **Medical validation.** All health content is reviewed by APSAD's President (medical doctor) before release.
5. **Quick exit.** One tap leaves the page for a neutral website.
6. **Human referral.** Every critical path ends with a referral to a health service, a helpline or a trusted adult.

Helpline numbers and clinic entries are placeholders until verified by APSAD.
